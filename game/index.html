TIDEBOUND

You are the last Tidekeeper. Ink-creatures are rising from the deep to swallow the light of the bay. You have no sword — only your tidal pulse. Push the ink back, gather the pearls they drop, and survive all 5 waves to wake the dawn.

W A S D / ← ↑ ↓ →moveSPACE / Clicktidal pulsePpause

Pulse knocks enemies back and shatters them — but it has a cooldown. Pearls raise your combo: the longer your chain, the more each pearl is worth. Between waves, choose one tide-gift to grow stronger. Beware the Tide Leviathan on wave 5.

HOW TO PLAY

🌀 Move — WASD or arrow keys (mouse / touch-drag also steers).
💥 Pulse — Space or click: a shockwave that damages and shoves every creature near you.
🐚 Pearls — creatures drop pearls. Swim through them to collect; chains raise your combo.
👾 Enemies — Drifters crawl at you · Darters lunge · Splitters burst into two minis.
✦ Between waves — pick 1 of 3 tide-gifts: stronger pulse, faster swim, magnet, armour…
🏆 Win — defeat the Tide Leviathan on wave 5.  💀 Lose — your 5 hearts run out.
